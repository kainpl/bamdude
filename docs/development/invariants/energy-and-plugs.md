# Invariants: Energy, smart plugs and sensors

Full text of the rules that [`CLAUDE.md`](../../../CLAUDE.md) keeps in short form, with the reasons and the history behind them. `Vault:` pointers name notes in the maintainer's private knowledge base; everything a contributor needs is here.

## Every automatic plug switch goes through one seam

`smart_plug_manager.plug_switched(plug, on, *, scripts=…)` announces the switch to the relay and runs the printer's Home Assistant scripts by the rule its caller names (`automatic` / `manual` / `none` — no default, so a new site must choose); a guard test requires it at every switch site. Automatic runs are for the plug that controls the printer's power only, once per (printer, direction) within two minutes, behind `farm_startup_guard` for ON and the caller's `may_run`.

## Per-print energy

`PrintArchive.energy_start_kwh` captured at print start (helper `_record_energy_start` in `main.py`); end-handler `_record_print_energy` (module-level, lifted out of `on_print_complete` to be testable) reads from fresh DB session, computes delta. **The END read is forced** — `_get_plug_energy(..., force_read=True)` → `service.refresh(plug)` for drivers flagged `reads_from_a_cache` (Zigbee only; HTTP/MQTT drivers are already live). The plug's counter moves only when it reports, ≥30 s apart, so an unforced final read systematically drops the last half-minute of a print, always in the same direction. Start is deliberately NOT forced: the same lag there covers an idle printer and cannot pay for a radio read on the dispatch path. The flag routes via a driver attribute, never `plug_type` — that if/elif is what `_get_plug_energy` exists to have removed. Restart-resilient — never in-memory dicts. Range stats via hourly snapshots in `smart_plug_energy_snapshots` + `_sum_snapshot_deltas()` in `archives.py`.

## The electricity price has a history with one writer, and money is worked out at the price of its own time (m245)

`energy_price_history` (every change of `energy_cost_per_kwh`, with its moment and source; a `baseline` row far in the past carries the price of the upgrade day) is written only by `services/energy_price.py` — the seam is `routes/settings.set_setting`, which every door passes (settings save, `POST /settings/electricity-price`, reset, Git restore, the Home Assistant read), and a guard test fails any other writer. A print is costed along its own source's counter points (start reading, the plug's admitted snapshots inside the window, end reading), each step at the price in effect at its START (`cost_of_steps`); an estimated print at the time-weighted average over its window; `energy_provenance.tariff` is the effective average and `tariff_method` says how (`steps` / `window_average` / `single`). The plug-level cost in statistics is built from the same snapshots the kWh comes from, never from today's price. A settled figure is never re-costed, and nothing here reads the network on the print path: the Home Assistant price (`energy_price_source = homeassistant`) is read once an hour in the snapshot loop and at the settings save, with the credential rule's order (freeze the destination, close the transaction, then network, then a new short transaction). ⚠️ Do not port upstream's running totals on snapshots — they collide with the meter-source rotation and the two snapshot writers. Vault: `40-invariants/inv-energy-price-history-one-writer.md`.

## A stalled plug counter is a suspicion; a print's estimate is marked, never a zero and never a total (m211, plug-meter-watchdog r4 + reviews r1–r2)

The lifetime counter stays the measurement until the power history of the SAME measurement source proves it flat under load: `meter_detector` — a flat run of ≥ 2 snapshots with no snapshot gap > 2 h is proven when ANY stretch of it between two of its counter points, ≥ 30 min long, is covered (`power_integrator`, ≥ 95 %, no extrapolation, a marked point makes both its segments unusable) with at least the plug's threshold (100 Wh, an override only raises it) — the energy is a condition of choosing the stretch, never a test of the longest one, so neither an old hole nor a long quiet stretch hides a heavy one; the integrals are taken per neighbouring pair over a slice of the history and compared through prefix sums, never re-integrated per candidate. That arithmetic (normalise, detect, integrate) runs off the event loop through `asyncio.to_thread`, and a window holding more than `MAX_POWER_ROWS` power rows is refused by a COUNT before any row is read (`BudgetExceeded` → `insufficient_budget`, never a partial sum as a whole one). The first rise after an episode's last flat point closes it — read off the counter points (`rise_after`), never off a run the current window proves again; a reset followed by a step counts, a reset alone and silence do not — neighbouring plugs prove nothing. `meter_watch` holds one active episode per plug (partial unique index) and raises one in-app-only `plug_meter_stalled` per episode in the episode's own transaction; the sweep re-reads its whole basis (`watch_basis`: token, cutoff, threshold, MQTT interval) under `source_guard` before it writes an episode or its cursor, and the plug route writes a rotation or a policy change under the same lock, resetting the cursor in SQL (`reset_watch_cursor` — an attribute set to the value the session loaded is no change to the ORM); a print opens an episode only under the basis it was assessed by; and an episode of a discarded source is closed without waiting for a counter point and never listed; nothing is ever sent to the device. `_record_print_energy` integrates the WHOLE measured window once (stored baseline moment → the archive's end, never now) when it meets a proven run or an active episode the counter did not leave before the start; too little history, an unknown end (reconciliation), a changed source or an end read at or before the source's cutoff (`clock_before_source`) is NULL with a reason. Every figure it settles is final (`energy_provenance.final`, measured included) and an operator's reconstruction (`energy_reconstructed_from` without provenance) is settled too (`_energy_settled`); `_write_print_energy` re-reads the source under `source_guard` — taken before the archive's locks and SQLite's writer, the order the flush and a rotation keep — so a rotation during the assessment writes `source_changed`. The source is a token + strict cutoff on the plug row (`meter_source`), and ONE rule, `admits` (same token, strictly after the cutoff — after the clock went back it may lie ahead of now), decides for the flush, every counter snapshot and the print's start and end; any identity-field change rotates it and closes the episode as `source_changed`, a rename does not. A received-but-untrusted reading is a row with `unusable_reason` (state unknown on a phantom-watts-risk source — Zigbee by default; invalid value; `buffer_loss`; `batch_unknown` when a commit's outcome is unknown — those markers wait in their own queue and overflow never evicts them, newer readings give way), never a dropped one; the chart's bucket holding one is NULL. Readers keep what the figure is: archive `energy_status` (`energy_wire_fields`), the statistics' and order figures' estimated/unavailable counts, the Git backup's `energy_record`; snapshots, lifetime and date-range totals never contain an estimate. Restore retires the buffer before the swap. Vault: `40-invariants/inv-plug-meter-stall-is-a-marked-estimate.md`.

## A switched-off plug reports 0 W, as physics — not as trust in the device

`zigbee/driver.py::get_energy` returns `power: 0.0` whenever the known state is `OFF`, whatever the reading cache holds; an unknown state is NOT treated as off. Cheap plugs keep reporting the last measured watts/amps after the relay opens (S60ZBTPF pre-2.0.3 firmware did exactly that, and BamDude showed 33 W for an empty socket). The upstream quirk fixes only transitions it witnesses, so it cannot cover a restart. `total` is never zero-filled — a fabricated zero would be differenced as real consumption. **Zigbee quirks need two conditions, both silent when broken:** `ControllerApplication.new()` must get `device_resolver=` (without it zigpy applies NO quirks at all), and attribute values must come from `cluster.get(attr)`, never from what `read_attributes` returned (that is the raw pre-quirk value, and zigpy suppresses the update event for the attribute being read). Verify with `GET /zigbee/devices/{ieee}/attributes` — `device_class` must be `CustomZigpyDevice`, and raw vs cached diverge exactly where a quirk acted. **Reports arrive on `on_event`, never on `add_listener` alone** — zigpy 2.x delivers a reported attribute inside `_suppress_attribute_update_event`, so the legacy channel hears nothing at all; `reporting.py::_subscribe_cluster` is the single place both channels are wired, and a listener needs `__call__(event)` as well as `attribute_updated`. A stub that only has `add_listener` will confirm whatever wrong model you hold (it did, for 300+ green tests). The 30–45 s poll (`zigbee/poller.py`) is the **floor**, not the mechanism — measured 35 value changes in 3 min from reports vs ~5 from polling — and it stays because ZHA polls `ElectricalMeasurement` by default too (exempting 4 allowlisted models), while for `Metering` the rule is inverted (no poll except 3 known-bad models). **Reporting bounds are per cluster, from ZHA, not one shared triple** (`_POLLED_CLUSTERS` carries a `_ReportingBounds` per row, pinned by `test_the_bounds_asked_of_each_cluster_are_zhas`): OnOff `0/900/1` (a relay cannot chatter by itself; a floor only delays the answer to our own command), Metering summation `30/900/1` (it only ever grows, so "changed by 1" holds continuously — at 5 s that is a message every few seconds all print long), ActivePower `5/900/1`. `min_interval` is the floor between two **device reports**, not a poll interval. Battery sensors are never polled: `power_class` reads `node_desc.is_receiver_on_when_idle`, **not** `getattr(flags, "RxOnWhenIdle")`, which returns the always-truthy IntFlag member.

## Measurement history is written ABOVE the plug drivers, never from a device listener

`services/measurement_history.py` (driven by `smart_plug_manager._history_loop`) is the only writer of `smart_plug_power_history`; sensors reach `smart_sensor_history` the same way through `services/measurement_buffer.py`. Writing from the Zigbee listener would make an application feature exist only for one of the five plug types. **Hybrid by necessity:** zigbee and mqtt report, so every report is written; tasmota, rest and home_assistant never report at all — their only readings today are the hourly energy snapshot and *the frontend polling while a tab is open*, so their history would record when somebody was watching. Those three are sampled by the loop instead. The accepted consequence is that series density differs by plug type. Buffer first, flush later: both report callbacks are **synchronous** and must not raise (zigpy swallows listener exceptions at DEBUG), so a direct DB write would be a coroutine nobody awaits. There is deliberately **no de-duplication** — a flat line and a lost connection must not look alike. Pruning rides in the same loop that samples, so writing and pruning cannot survive each other.

## The zigbee device-class set is closed

— smart plugs and sensors, nothing else. A new quantity is a ROW in `zigbee/measurements.py`, never a new class: the classes carry pairing, freshness and UI behaviour, and a third one means re-deciding all three for what is really just another reading.

## Sensor reporting has three outcomes, not two

`unanswered` (a sleeper that never woke — the usual case) ≠ `refused` (an explicit non-SUCCESS from the device) ≠ `ok`. Only a complete apply records the desired state; anything less is retried at the next contact, or one badly timed moment leaves a measurement unconfigured until re-pairing. Defaults live in `zigbee/measurements.py` (pinned by `test_the_defaults_are_the_agreed_ones`), taken from ZHA. `reportable_change` conversion branches on `raw_is_integer`: integer attributes floor at 1, floats must NOT — 1 ppm of CO2 is `0.000001` raw, and flooring it to 1 asks for a report every million ppm, i.e. silence forever.
